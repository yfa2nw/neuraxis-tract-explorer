How to Use Neuraxis Tract Explorer

1. Explore Tracts, Nerves & Nuclei

Click any pathway, cranial nerve (CN I–XII), or brain nucleus in the left directory—or click directly on any structure inside a cross-section slice—to select it. Hover to preview an item; click to pin it. A pinned pathway filters the view to the slices where it is present (use ⤢ Spotlight on any slice to enlarge it).

2. Search by Anatomy or Clinical Syndrome

Press / to search across tracts, sub-nuclei (e.g., Nucleus ambiguus, Edinger-Westphal), or clinical syndromes (e.g., Wallenberg, Weber, Brown-Séquard, INO, Horner).

2b. Lesion Syndromes

Pick a spinal cord lesion (central cord, anterior cord, posterior cord, Brown-Séquard) or a medullary stroke (lateral medullary / Wallenberg, medial medullary / Dejerine) from the Spinal cord lesions and Brainstem strokes groups or the Lesions chip. The affected slice shades the lesion, marks damaged tracts in red, and the inspector lists the cause, deficits, bedside tests (How to check) and imaging. Unilateral lesions have a Left / Right toggle.

3. 3D Brain & 2D Longitudinal Schematic

Drag on the 3D Brain to orbit, scroll or use − / + to zoom, and adjust the 3D Opacity slider to see deep subcortical nuclei and 3D fiber pathways. Click any cutting plane in the 3D brain or level block in the 2D schematic to jump straight to that slice.

4. Slice Modes, Laterality & Decussations

Switch between Vector, Weigert Stain, MRI (T1w), and Luxol Blue in the top bar. Toggle Pathway Side vs. Bilateral to see how ascending and descending tracts cross at decussation levels (marked with ✕).

5. Spotlight & Resizable Panels

Click ⤢ Spotlight (or any slice title) to expand that slice across the top of the grid. You can also click and drag the edges of the sidebar, the 3D/2D column, the inspector card, or any slice card to resize them (double-click any edge to reset).

6. 🎓 Quiz Me! Mode

Click 🎓 Quiz Me! in the header to test yourself! Choose which anatomical slices to include, toggle ascending/descending direction animations on or off, and pick between Multiple Choice, Type the Name, or Label All on Slice.
